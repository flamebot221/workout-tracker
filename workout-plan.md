## Jeff Nippard Scientific Principles Applied to This Plan

1. **Stretch-Mediated Hypertrophy (Lengthened Overload):**
   - Research shows loaded stretching under high tension triggers superior muscle hypertrophy. Exercises like Seated Leg Curls, Incline Bench, Pec Deck Flyes, Cable Overhead Triceps Extensions, and Bayesian Curls are prioritized to overload the muscle when fully stretched.
2. **Proximity to Failure & RIR (Reps in Reserve):**
   - **Compound Lifts (Bench, Incline Barbell, Barbell RDL, Pendlay Row):** Stop sets with **1–2 RIR** (technical failure). Never break form on axial/spinal loaded movements.
   - **Isolation & Machine Movements:** Train to **0–1 RIR**.
   - **Designated Failure Sets:** Where written as *"to failure"* (Smith Squats Set 3, Friday Leg Extensions Both Sets, Hip Thrusts Set 2, Leg Press Top Set), push until you cannot complete another concentric rep with full range of motion.
3. **Double Progression System:**
   - Stay within the prescribed rep range. When you reach the top of the range on **all sets** of an exercise with pristine form, add the smallest available weight increment next session.
4. **Pull-Up Progression Protocol:**
   - Because full bodyweight pull-ups are not yet attainable, use **Heavy Strict Lat Pulldowns** (or Assisted Pull-up Machine) in the 6–8 rep bracket with a 2-second eccentric phase. As vertical pulling strength increases and body weight drops toward 85 kg, transition to negative-only pull-ups, then unassisted bodyweight pull-ups, and eventually weighted pull-ups.

## Monday — Upper Body
*Evening Gym: 8:30 PM • 75–90 min*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Equipment | Jeff Nippard Form Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Incline Barbell Bench Press** | 3 × 6–8 | 2–2.5 min | Barbell + Incline Bench (15–30°) | Set bench to a modest 15–30° incline (not steep 45° to spare front delts). Pinch shoulder blades down and back. Lower with 2–3s control to upper chest below collarbones; press up and slightly back without flaring elbows past 60°. |
| 2 | **Seated Cable Fly / Pec Deck** | 2 × 10–12 | 75–90 sec | Cable Machine / Pec Deck | Set handles at mid-chest level. Keep scapulae locked into the pad throughout; never roll shoulders forward. Pause for 1 full second in deep stretch, then squeeze biceps together across chest. |
| 3 | **Lat Pulldown (Pull-Up Progression)** | 3 × 6–8 | 2 min | Lat Pulldown Machine | Sit tall with slight 10–15° lean. Depress shoulder blades down first, then drive elbows straight into your hip pockets. 2–3s controlled negative to full lat stretch at top. |
| 4 | **High Cable / DB Lateral Raise** | 2 × 8–10 | 75 sec | Cable Pulley or Dumbbells | Set pulley at hip/wrist height. Raise arms 15–30° in front of body (in the scapular plane). Think of "scooping" the weight away toward the walls rather than straight up. |
| 5 | **Pendlay Row** | 2 × 8–10 | 2 min | Barbell + Plates | Torso locked completely parallel to floor, neutral spine. The bar begins at a dead stop on the floor on every single rep. Explosively pull to lower chest/sternum; do not heave with hips. |
| 6 | **Cable Overhead Triceps Extension** | 2 × 8–10 | 75 sec | Cable Station + Rope | Cable set at chest/hip height; lean forward 30°. Keep elbows pointed comfortably forward. Let hands sink behind head for deep long-head triceps stretch; lock forearms out under control. |
| 7 | **Bayesian Curl / Preacher Curl** | 2 × 8–10 | 75 sec | Cable Pulley or Preacher Bench | For Bayesian curl: stand facing away from low pulley with shoulder extended behind torso for maximum bicep stretch. Maintain upper arm position and curl forward. |

## Tuesday — Lower Body (Legs 1: Squat & Curl Focus)
*Evening Gym: 8:30 PM • 75–90 min*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Equipment | Jeff Nippard Form Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Leg Curl** | 3 × 10–15 | 90 sec | Seated Leg Curl Machine | Pre-exhaust hamstrings and lubricate knees. Hinge torso slightly forward to put hamstrings on a deep stretch. Curl down smoothly, pause at peak contraction, and resist eccentric for 2–3s. |
| 2 | **Smith Machine Squat** | 3 × 5–10 | 2.5 min | Smith Machine | Place feet 3–6 inches in front of bar line. Allows an upright torso and massive quad knee flexion with zero lumbar shear. Squat deep; **LAST SET TO FAILURE**. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift (RDL)** | 3 × 5–10 | 2 min | Barbell or DBs + Straps | Use lifting straps to bypass grip limitations. Soft knee bend (~15–20°), then push hips straight back to the wall behind you. Keep bar dragging down shins; stop once hips stop moving back. Flat back throughout. |
| 4 | **Leg Extension** | 3 × 10–15 | 75 sec | Leg Extension Machine | Set backrest back to open hip angle and stretch the rectus femoris. Grip side handles firmly to anchor pelvis down into seat. Squeeze quads hard at peak extension. |
| 5 | **Standing Calf Raise** | 4 × 10–15 | 60 sec | Smith Machine or DBs + Step | Lower heels into a full stretch and **PAUSE for 1–2 full seconds** at the bottom to kill tendon stretch reflex. Drive through balls of big toes to full peak height. |

## Thursday — Push
*Evening Gym: 8:30 PM • 75–90 min*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Equipment | Jeff Nippard Form Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Flat Bench Press** | 3 × 8–10 | 2–2.5 min | Bench Station + Barbell | Modest arch, shoulder blades pinched and tucked. Lower bar under control and **PAUSE for 1 full second on lower chest** to kill bounce and build explosive pec strength. |
| 2 | **Machine Shoulder Press** | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min | Plate-Loaded / Selectorized Press | Adjust seat so handles begin at chin level. Tuck elbows ~30° forward in the scapular plane (never flare wide at 90°). Press up smoothly without hyperextending lumbar spine. |
| 3 | **Pec Deck Fly** | 3 × 15–20 | 75 sec | Pec Deck Machine | Adjust arms to allow as deep a stretch as comfortably possible without anterior shoulder roll. Keep chest high, sweep handles forward, and squeeze inner chest fibers together. |
| 4 | **Cable Lateral Raise** | 3 × 10–15 | 75 sec | Cable Pulley + Handle | Pulley at hip height. Scoop the weight outward and away from torso in scapular plane (~20° forward). Constant tension throughout; 2s eccentric lowering. |
| 5 | **Overhead Cable Triceps Extension** | 3 × 5–10 | 90 sec | Cable Station + Rope | Heavy load overload. Lean forward with braced core. Allow deep stretch behind neck, then powerfully extend forearms without elbow flare. |
| 6 | **Triceps Cable Kickback** | 3 × 10–15 | 60 sec | Cable Station (no handle/cuff) | Set cable at hip height. Hinge forward 45°, pin upper arm parallel to torso (or slightly behind body). Extend forearm fully back into peak triceps contraction; hold for 1s. |

| 5 | **Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise** | 2 × 8–10 | 60 sec | Heavy Dumbbells + Step/Plates | Hold heavy dumbbells at sides. Pause for 2 full seconds in the deep bottom stretch on every single rep, then explode onto balls of feet. |

## Saturday — Pull
*Evening Gym: 8:30 PM • 75–90 min*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Equipment | Jeff Nippard Form Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Close-Grip Lat Pulldown** | 3 × 10–15 | 90–100 sec | Lat Pulldown + V-Grip/Neutral | Keep torso nearly vertical at top for maximum lat stretch. Initiate by depressing scapulae and driving elbows down tight to your sides directly into your pockets. |
| 2 | **Incline Chest-Supported DB Row** *(Machine Substitute)* | 3 × 10–12 | 90 sec | 30–45° Incline Bench + DBs | Optimal mid-back & mid-traps builder without lower back strain. Flare elbows 45–60° and pinch shoulder blades together at the top for 1 full second. |
| 3 | **Close-Grip Seated Cable Row** | 2 × 15–20 | 75 sec | Seated Cable Row + V-Grip | Sit upright with chest tall. Let shoulders protract slightly at full stretch, then pull handle toward navel, keeping elbows tucked close to ribcage for lower lat fibers. |
| 4 | **Reverse Cable Fly** | 3 × 15–20 | 60 sec | Functional Trainer (Pulleys at eye level) | Remove handles and hold cable balls. Keep weight light and reps high (15–20) so mid-traps do not take over. Pull out in a wide horizontal arc focusing strictly on rear deltoids. |
| 5 | **Dumbbell or Trap-Bar Shrugs** *(Machine Substitute)* | 4 × 15–20 | 60 sec | Dumbbells or Trap/Hex Bar | Upper trap fibers run diagonally outward. Lean torso forward ~10° and shrug up and slightly backward toward your ears. Hold the peak contraction for 1–2 seconds on every rep. |
| 6 | **EZ-Bar Curl** | 3 × 10–15 | 75 sec | EZ Curl Bar + Plates | Semi-supinated grip on EZ bar protects wrists while maximizing bicep tension. Pin elbows to ribs; do not swing torso or let elbows drift excessively forward. |
| 7 | **Preacher Curl (Machine or EZ-Bar/DB)** | 3 × 15–20 | 60 sec | Preacher Bench + EZ-Bar / DBs / Machine | Targets the short head of the biceps with zero shoulder cheating. Keep armpits snug against the top of the pad. Curl up smoothly and lower with strict 2–3s eccentric control. |
