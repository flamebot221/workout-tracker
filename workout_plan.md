## Jeff Nippard Scientific Principles Applied to This Plan

1. **Stretch-Mediated Hypertrophy (Lengthened Overload):**
   - Research shows loaded stretching under high tension triggers superior muscle hypertrophy. Exercises like Seated Leg Curls, Smith Incline Bench, Pec Deck Flyes, Cable Overhead Triceps Extensions, and Preacher Curls are prioritized to overload the muscle when fully stretched.
2. **Proximity to Failure & RIR (Reps in Reserve):**
   - **Compound Lifts (Smith Bench, Barbell RDL, Smith Hip Thrust, Leg Press):** Stop sets with **1–2 RIR** (technical failure) while perfecting form. Never break form on axial/spinal loaded movements.
   - **Isolation & Machine Movements:** Train to **0–1 RIR**.
   - **Designated Failure Sets:** Where written as *"to failure"* (Smith Squats Set 3, Friday Leg Extensions Both Sets), push until you cannot complete another concentric rep with full range of motion.
3. **Double Progression System:**
   - Stay within the prescribed rep range. When you reach the top of the range on **all sets** of an exercise with pristine form, add the smallest available weight increment next session.
4. **Pull-Up Progression Protocol & Grip Assistance:**
   - Because full bodyweight pull-ups are not yet attainable, use **Heavy Strict Lat Pulldowns** (or Assisted Pull-up Machine) in the 6–8 rep bracket with a 2-second eccentric phase.
   - **Use Lifting Straps + Thumbless ("Hook") Grip:** Wrap lifting straps around the bar/handle and place your thumbs *on top* of the bar alongside your index fingers. Treat your hands as passive hooks to prevent forearms and biceps from taking over.

## Monday — Upper Body
*Evening Gym: 8:30 PM • 75–90 min*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Equipment | Personalized Form Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Smith Machine Incline Bench Press** *(Replaces Free Barbell)* | 3 × 6–8 | 2–2.5 min | Smith Machine + Incline Bench (15–30°) | Fixed bar path prevents forward drifting. Set bench so bar touches upper chest just below collarbones. Pinch shoulder blades down and back; keep elbows tucked at ~45–60°. |
| 2 | **Seated Cable Fly / Pec Deck** | 2 × 10–12 | 75–90 sec | Cable Machine / Pec Deck | Set handles at mid-chest level. Keep scapulae locked into the pad throughout; never roll shoulders forward. Pause for 1 full second in deep stretch, then squeeze biceps together across chest. |
| 3 | **Lat Pulldown (Pull-Up Progression)** | 3 × 6–8 | 2 min | Lat Pulldown Machine + Straps | **2-Step Cue:** First keep arms straight and pull shoulders *down* away from ears (scapular pull-down). Second, drive elbows down toward hip pockets. Stop at collarbone height so shoulders do not roll forward. |
| 4 | **High Cable / DB Lateral Raise** | 2 × 8–10 | 75 sec | Cable Pulley or Dumbbells | Actively push shoulders **down** toward the floor before starting to prevent upper traps from shrugging. Push knuckles *outward toward the walls* in the scapular plane (20° forward) and stop at shoulder height. |
| 5 | **Chest-Supported Machine Row / T-Bar Row** *(Replaces Pendlay Row)* | 2 × 8–10 | 2 min | Chest-Supported Row Machine + Straps | Eliminates lower-back rounding while targeting the exact same mid-back fibers. Keep chest glued to pad, travel elbows at a **45° angle** to torso, and squeeze shoulder blades together at peak. |
| 6 | **Cable Overhead Triceps Extension** | 2 × 8–10 | 75 sec | Cable Station + Rope | Cable set at chest/hip height; lean forward 30°. Keep elbows pointed comfortably forward. Let hands sink behind head for deep long-head triceps stretch; lock forearms out under control. |
| 7 | **Preacher Curl** *(Replaces Bayesian Curl)* | 2 × 8–10 | 75 sec | Preacher Bench + EZ-Bar / Machine | Locks elbows in place to prevent anterior shoulder strain. Keep armpits snug against top of pad, curl smoothly, and lower with a strict 2–3s eccentric stretch. |

## Tuesday — Lower Body (Legs 1: Squat & Curl Focus)
*Evening Gym: 8:30 PM • 75–90 min*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Equipment | Personalized Form Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Leg Curl** | 3 × 10–15 | 90 sec | Seated Leg Curl Machine | Pre-exhaust hamstrings and lubricate knees. Hinge torso slightly forward to put hamstrings on a deep stretch. Curl down smoothly, pause at peak contraction, and resist eccentric for 2–3s. |
| 2 | **Smith Machine Squat** | 3 × 5–10 | 2.5 min | Smith Machine | Place feet 3–6 inches in front of bar line. Allows an upright torso and massive quad knee flexion with zero lumbar shear. Squat deep; **LAST SET TO FAILURE**. |
| 3 | **Romanian Deadlift (RDL)** | 3 × 5–10 | 2 min | Barbell or DBs + Straps | Push hips straight back toward the wall behind you rather than bending your upper body down. Stop as soon as hips stop moving back (slight natural knee bend of 15–20° is fine). Flat back throughout. |
| 4 | **Leg Extension** | 3 × 10–15 | 75 sec | Leg Extension Machine | Set backrest back to open hip angle and stretch the rectus femoris. Grip side handles firmly to anchor pelvis down into seat. Squeeze quads hard at peak extension. |
| 5 | **Standing Calf Raise** | 4 × 10–15 | 60 sec | Smith Machine or DBs + Step | Lower heels into a full stretch and **PAUSE for 1–2 full seconds** at the bottom to kill tendon stretch reflex. Drive through balls of big toes to full peak height. |

## Thursday — Push
*Evening Gym: 8:30 PM • 75–90 min*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Equipment | Personalized Form Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Smith Machine Flat Bench Press** *(Replaces Free Barbell)* | 3 × 8–10 | 2–2.5 min | Smith Machine + Flat Bench | Locks in the bar path so you can focus purely on maintaining your chest arch and scapular retraction. | 2 | **Machine Shoulder Press** | 3 × 10–12 | 2 min | Plate-Loaded / Selectorized Press | Adjust seat so handles begin at chin level. Tuck elbows ~30° forward in the scapular plane (never flare wide at 90°). Press up smoothly without hyperextending lumbar spine. |

| 4 | **Cable Lateral Raise** | 3 × 10–15 | 75 sec | Cable Pulley + Handle | Pulley at hip height. Keep shoulders depressed down away from ears; sweep knuckles outward toward the walls in the scapular plane (~20° forward). Stop at shoulder level; 2s lowering. |
| 5 | **Overhead Cable Triceps Extension** | 3 × 5–10 | 90 sec | Cable Station + Rope | Heavy load overload. Lean forward with braced core. Allow deep stretch behind neck, then powerfully extend forearms without elbow flare. |
| 6 | **V-Bar Cable Triceps Pushdown** *(Replaces Cable Kickbacks)* | 3 × 10–15 | 60 sec | Cable Station + V-Bar | Pin elbows tightly to your ribs. Press the V-bar down until arms are fully locked out at the bottom, squeeze triceps for 1 second, and control the return to 90° elbow flexion. |

## Saturday — Pull
*Evening Gym: 8:30 PM • 75–90 min*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Equipment | Personalized Form Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Close-Grip Lat Pulldown** | 3 × 10–15 | 90–100 sec | Lat Pulldown + V-Grip/Neutral + Straps | Use lifting straps with a thumbless grip. Depress shoulder blades down *before* bending elbows. Drive elbows tight to sides toward your pockets and stop at upper chest. |
| 2 | **Incline Chest-Supported DB Row** *(Machine Substitute)* | 3 × 10–12 | 90 sec | 30–45° Incline Bench + DBs + Straps | Optimal mid-back & mid-traps builder without lower back strain. Flare elbows 45–60° and pinch shoulder blades together at the top for 1 full second. |
| 3 | **Close-Grip Seated Cable Row** | 2 × 15–20 | 75 sec | Seated Cable Row + V-Grip + Straps | Sit upright with chest tall. Let shoulders protract slightly at full stretch, then pull handle toward navel, keeping elbows tucked close to ribcage for lower lat fibers. |
| 4 | **Reverse Cable Fly** | 3 × 15–20 | 60 sec | Functional Trainer (Pulleys at eye level) | Keep using your preferred rear-delt variation! Keep weight light and reps high (15–20) so mid-traps do not take over; focus strictly on sweeping arms outward with rear delts. |
| 5 | **Dumbbell or Trap-Bar Shrugs** *(Machine Substitute)* | 4 × 15–20 | 60 sec | Dumbbells or Trap/Hex Bar + Straps | Upper trap fibers run diagonally outward. Lean torso forward ~10° and shrug up and slightly backward toward your ears. Hold the peak contraction for 1–2 seconds on every rep. |
| 6 | **EZ-Bar Curl** | 3 × 10–15 | 75 sec | EZ Curl Bar + Plates | Semi-supinated grip on EZ bar protects wrists while maximizing bicep tension. Pin elbows to ribs; do not swing torso or let elbows drift excessively forward. |
| 7 | **Preacher Curl (Machine or EZ-Bar/DB)** | 3 × 15–20 | 60 sec | Preacher Bench + EZ-Bar / DBs / Machine | Targets the short head of the biceps with zero shoulder cheating. Keep armpits snug against the top of the pad. Curl up smoothly and lower with strict 2–3s eccentric control. |
